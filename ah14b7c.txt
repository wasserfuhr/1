1. THE CORE IDEA
----------------
Standard computers measure time in UNIX seconds since 1970.
AlphaTime (at) resets the clock to a specific shared epoch:

    [28. Sep 2015 - Blood Moon / Q5817057]  <--  Epoch Zero (at0)

Every moment after that is measured in hex-encoded seconds elapsed
since at0.

    at0         = 0x0        (Sep 28, 2015 - Genesis)
    at14b7ca72  = 0x14b7ca72 (Oct 3, 2026 - Present Moment)


2. WHY HEX SECONDS?
-------------------
- Compact: Fits easily into filenames, code tags, and prompts.
- Deterministic: Acts as an immutable timestamp for logs (dd*.txt).
- Human + Machine Readable: Serves as a uniform state marker across
  different LLM runs, repositories, and automated processes.
